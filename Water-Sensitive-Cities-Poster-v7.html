What is a water-sensitive city?

A city that manages rain, rivers, groundwater and wastewater as one cycle, storing rain where it falls instead of draining it away.

Pipes and parks only work when institutions, access and maintenance change with them.

Urban water systems are shaped in three registers

City water is shaped by people and capital, not rainfall alone. Buried pipes can hide who was never connected.

Delhi

One month, two water crises
NCT, India

Flooded street with rickshaws after heavy rain

Flood
228 mm in 24 hours · 28 June 2024
Loss
July · basement deaths, Old Rajinder Nagar

Technical cause

  • Most raw water comes from other states
  • Drains are covered and clogged

What decided it

  • Supply contested across state lines
  • Water moves by political pressure

Who carried the risk

  • Poorer zones and unauthorised colonies
  • Basements carried the deadliest risk

Zhengzhou

When sponge design meets extreme rain
Henan, China

Zhengzhou street flooding, July 2021 Henan floods

Rain
201.9 mm in one hour · 20 July 2021
Deaths
380 dead or missing

Technical cause

  • Sponge measures weaken as storms intensify
  • No safe overflow route for metro or tunnels

What decided it

  • Warnings ignored; deaths concealed
  • Sponge budgets went to landscaping

Who carried the risk

  • Metro and tunnel users
  • Residents sold a climate-hero story
