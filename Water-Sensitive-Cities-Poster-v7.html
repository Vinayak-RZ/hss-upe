How an urban water system fails

A pipe, a budget and a claim on land are one system.

Technocratic

How water is moved, stored and drained.

  • A drain or a tanker can hide a broken lake.
  • Lakes, sewers and groundwater are planned apart.
  • Zhengzhou took 201.9 mm in one hour.

Bureaucratic

Who is responsible, who funds and who maintains.

  • Delhi’s water policy has waited since 2011.
  • No one office can halt a metro and clear a drain.
  • Sponge money went to landscaping, not drains.

Political ecology

Who gets the water, who is exposed, and who is heard.

  • Shortage and flood are blamed on the people in them.
  • Tankers and borewells sit outside the plan.
  • Deaths and losses can go uncounted.

Political and social

Common problems

  • Lakes, drains and sponge parks are built for a milder rain than the city now gets.
  • Upkeep, warnings and a water policy sit with offices that do not act together.
  • Flood and shortage fall on settlers, basement users and people left uncounted.

Solutions

Pipes, offices, and who the water reaches.

Technocratic

  • Reconnect the lakes so rain has a path downhill and floodplains can soak.
  • Meter each district and find leaks before promising a new supply.
  • Keep green streets, and add drains that still empty in an extreme storm.

Bureaucratic

  • Put lakes, drains and sewage under one authority, and fund the upkeep.
  • Pass the water policy waiting since 2011, and name who carries it out.
  • A red alert should stop the metro before people are underground.

Political ecology

  • Secure tenure before clearance, and enforce the rule on the powerful too.
  • Count tankers and borewells as supply, and even it out across zones.
  • Publish deaths, shortages and flood maps while the event is still news.
